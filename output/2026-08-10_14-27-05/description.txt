West Bengal has announced a monthly pension of Rs 10,000 for 325 Emergency-era protesters who have been officially recognized as Loktantara Senani for their sacrifices. In addition to the financial support, these individuals will receive free travel on government buses and comprehensive medical assistance. During the announcement, the state leadership emphasized the vital importance of preserving nationalism and Indian culture for the younger generation, while also highlighting ongoing government commitments to strengthening border security and ending appeasement politics.
