UEFA aur doosri football associations ne ek baar phir FIFA president Gianni Infantino ki aalochana ki hai. Ek naye open letter mein, in sabhi bodies ne sports ki governing body mein ek fresh start yaani nayi shuruwat ki maang ki hai. Yeh letter Monday ko publish kiya gaya tha, jisme FIFA ke leadership par sawaal uthaye gaye hain aur badlaav laane par zor diya gaya hai.
