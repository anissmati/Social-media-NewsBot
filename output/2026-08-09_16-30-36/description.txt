Britain is taking bold steps to restore its declining native oyster populations, which have plummeted by a staggering 95% since the 1800s. To revive these vital marine ecosystems, conservation efforts are underway to drop 20 specially designed concrete reef cubes and an impressive 44 tons of scallop shells into the sea. These materials will provide the ideal hard surfaces needed for baby oysters to settle, grow, and rebuild thriving underwater habitats.
